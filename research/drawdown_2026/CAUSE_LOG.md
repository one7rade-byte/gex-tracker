| Leaders' prices | **Warning** | NVDA fell on a beat-and-raise; SMH −33% from its high |
| Governance | **New risk** | NVDA CEO exit |

**Read (2026-11-19):** so far this looks like a **financing-and-rates squeeze on an expensive leader group**, not a collapse in AI demand. The demand data is still strong. What has changed is the price of money (5%+ yields, a hike coming, wider AI credit spreads) and the supply of AI stock and debt being sold. That is also how bubbles usually start to deflate: prices fall while earnings are still good. The line between "correction" and "pop" is in the scoreboard: watch for capex cuts or guidance cuts, AI/HY credit breaking (HYG/IEF 20d ≤ −3%), and equal-weight/small caps falling as hard as tech.
